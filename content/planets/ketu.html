☋ Ketu

Evergreen notes • Lunar node • Vedic astrology

Mythology & Stories

Ketu is the south lunar node in Vedic astrology, paired with Rahu in the eclipse myth. If Rahu is the immortal head, Ketu is the body: instinctive, spiritual, strange, and difficult to pin down.

Food & Flavors

  • Simple, earthy, minimal flavors.
  • Herbal teas, roots, grains, and quiet comfort foods.
  • Drink pairing: warm ginger tea or a clean, unsweetened infusion.

Entertaining Bits

Ketu is linked with release, intuition, detachment, past-life patterns, and spiritual insight. Meme-able: “Ketu left the group chat before the argument started.”
