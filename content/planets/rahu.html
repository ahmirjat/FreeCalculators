☊ Rahu

Evergreen notes • Lunar node • Vedic astrology

Mythology & Stories

Rahu is the north lunar node in Vedic astrology, often described through the story of a shadow being who tasted the nectar of immortality. The Sun and Moon exposed the trick, but Rahu remained powerful as a head without a body, forever chasing them across the sky.

Food & Flavors

  • Smoky, unusual, or experimental flavors.
  • Street-food energy: bold, layered, and a little unpredictable.
  • Drink pairing: spiced tea or something with a dark, mysterious finish.

Entertaining Bits

Rahu is linked with appetite, ambition, obsession, foreignness, technology, and sudden leaps. Meme-able: “Rahu saw the limit and asked where the shortcut was.”
